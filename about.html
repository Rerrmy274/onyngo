Who We Are

Stamford Auto Care is a trusted automotive repair and maintenance garage conveniently located on Mombasa Road, behind ICD, Nairobi. We specialize in professional vehicle servicing, mechanical repairs, diagnostics, and preventive maintenance for all vehicle types.

The garage is led by Stephen Midiny, a highly experienced Automotive Engineer with over 20 years of industry expertise. His deep technical knowledge ensures accurate diagnostics, quality repairs, and durable automotive solutions.

At Stamford Auto Care, we are committed to professionalism, transparency, and customer satisfaction. By combining modern diagnostic tools with proven engineering practices, we deliver reliable, safe, and cost-effective auto care services.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, high-quality automotive repair and maintenance services through professional engineering expertise, honest workmanship, and customer-focused solutions.

Our Vision

To become a leading and trusted auto care garage in Nairobi by setting the standard for technical excellence, integrity, and dependable automotive service.
